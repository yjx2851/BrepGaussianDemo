BrepGaussian

CAD reconstruction from Multi-View Images with Gaussian Splatting

Jiaxing Yu, Dongyang Ren, Hangyu Xu, Zhouyuxiao Yang, Yuanqi Li, Jie Guo, Zhengkang Zhou, Yanwen Guo

State Key Laboratory of Novel Software Technology, Nanjing University

arXiv PDF GitHub
Given multi-view images of a CAD object, BrepGaussian reconstructs a B-Rep CAD model through feature-aware Gaussian Splatting and parametric surface fitting. The learned Gaussian representation produces geometry with edge and patch labels, enabling reconstruction of surfaces, curves, and final boundary representations.

Abstract

The boundary representation (B-Rep) models a 3D solid as its explicit boundaries: trimmed corners, edges, and faces. Recovering B-Rep representation from unstructured data is a challenging and valuable task of computer vision and graphics. We propose B-Rep Gaussian Splatting (BrepGaussian), a novel framework that learns 3D parametric representations from 2D images. We employ a Gaussian Splatting renderer with learnable features, followed by a specific fitting strategy. To disentangle geometry reconstruction and feature learning, we introduce a two-stage learning framework that first captures geometry and edges and then refines patch features to achieve clean geometry and coherent instance representations. Extensive experiments demonstrate the superior performance of our approach to state-of-the-art methods.
